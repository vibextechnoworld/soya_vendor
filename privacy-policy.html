Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how TBSPL Purchase Point ("we", "our", "the App") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

The App collects the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, Aadhaar number, PAN number, and bank account details of farmers for KYC and payment processing.
  • Identity Documents: Photographs of Aadhaar cards, PAN cards, driving licenses, 7/12 land documents, and bank passbooks.
  • Transaction Data: Purchase bills, weight details, quality analysis results, and payment records.
  • Location Data: Purchase point location for transaction records.
  • Device Information: Device model and Android OS version for app compatibility.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Farmer registration and KYC verification
  • Purchase bill generation and management
  • Quality analysis and deduction calculations
  • Payment processing and settlement
  • Report generation for business records
  • App functionality and technical support

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with:

  • Our company servers for secure storage and processing
  • Authorized purchase point operators for billing operations
  • Regulatory authorities if required by law

5. Data Security

All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols. Access to data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as necessary for business and legal compliance purposes. Users may request data deletion by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
